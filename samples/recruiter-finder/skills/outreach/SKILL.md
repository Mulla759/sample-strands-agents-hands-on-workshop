# Recruiter Outreach

Follow these steps when the user asks for an outreach message:

1. **Confirm the target.** Use `get_recruiter_details` to ground the message in real facts: the recruiter's name, agency, industry focus, and recent placements.

2. **Personalize the opener.** Reference something specific about the recruiter (a specialty, a recent placement, or their niche) — never a generic greeting.

3. **State who the user is in one line:** current or target role, years of experience, and the one thing that makes them stand out.

4. **Make one clear ask:** a 15-minute chat, feedback on fit, or being added to their pipeline. Pick one, not all three.

5. **Keep it under 150 words.** End with a polite close and the user's contact line.

## Important Notes
- Never promise outcomes (interviews, response rates, placements) — to the user or in the message.
- Use only details returned by the tools. Do not invent placements, awards, or preferences.
- If the recruiter has red flags (very low response rate, no niche), say so and suggest the user skip or deprioritize outreach.
- Suggest a single follow-up after ~5 business days; do not write more than one follow-up.
